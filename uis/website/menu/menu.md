# BRASALAND
### *Sabor a la Brasa de Nuestra Tierra*

Welcome to the official menu repository for Brasaland, an authentic Colombian charcoal-grill restaurant chain operating in Colombia and the USA.

---

## 🥟 ENTRADAS / APPETIZERS

* **Empanaditas de la Casa (4 units)**
  Crispy cornmeal pastries filled with shredded beef or charcoal-grilled chicken. Served with homemade ají (spicy salsa) and guacamole.
* **Chorizo Santarrosano a la Brasa**
  Grilled Colombian chorizo sausage served with warm sweet corn cake (*arepa de choclo*), melted farmer's cheese, and lime.
* **Chicharroncito Crocante**
  Crispy pork belly strips cooked over charcoal, served with green plantain chips (*patacones*) and *suero costeño* (Colombian sour cream).
* **Arepitas con Todo**
  Trio of mini white corn cakes topped with charcoal-grilled chicken, shredded beef, and *queso costeño*.

## 🥩 CORTES A LA BRASA / GRILL & STEAKS

* **Punta de Anca / Picanha a la Brasa (300g / 10oz)**
  Juicy top sirloin cap grilled to perfection and topped with house-made chimichurri.
* **Lomo al Trapo Tradicional (300g / 10oz)**
  Tender beef tenderloin wrapped in sea salt and herbs, cooked directly over glowing embers.
* **Costillas de Cerdo en BBQ de Panela**
  Smoked pork ribs glazed with our signature sugar-cane (*panela*) and passionfruit BBQ sauce.
* **Parrillada Brasaland (To Share / 2–3 Persons)**
  A feast featuring sirloin steak, grilled chicken breast, *chorizo*, *chicharrón*, blood sausage (*morcilla*), *patacones*, golden potatoes (*papa criolla*), and small *arepas*.

## 🥗 MENÚS LIGEROS & BOWLS / BOWLS & SALADS

* **Brasa Bowl**
  Coconut rice or white rice base, beans, grilled chicken or shredded beef, sweet plantains, corn, pico de gallo, and fresh avocado.
* **Ensalada del Campo a la Brasa**
  Mixed greens, grilled chicken breast, fresh cheese, sweet corn, cherry tomatoes, plantain strips, and passionfruit vinaigrette.
